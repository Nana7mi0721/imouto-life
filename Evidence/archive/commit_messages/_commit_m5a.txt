M5-A: fractional fit scaling and fullscreen

Add ScaleMode (integer / fit-nearest / fit-linear) and FitScaler so the
1024x576 canvas can fill a 16:9 window fractionally instead of being
limited to the retail integer grid. Renderer keeps separate nearest and
linear blit samplers, maps window points back through the active mode,
and play gains --scale/--fullscreen plus F9/F11 controls. Docs and
handover updated; no new dependencies.
